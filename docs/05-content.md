# Content and voice

All copy lives in `apps/web/src/features/marketing/content.ts`. Nothing user-facing is written inside a
component.

## Voice

Calm, plain, adult. Warm without being soft. It should read as though a
thoughtful person wrote it, not a marketing team.

Concretely:

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Plain words over product words: "start a conversation", not "initiate a
  match".
- Second person. Address one reader, not an audience.
- Understatement over enthusiasm. No exclamation marks anywhere.
- Acknowledge difficulty without dwelling on it. "Meeting someone new asks a lot
  of you" — then move to what we do about it.

## Words we do not use

**Dating-app clichés:** soulmate, spark, chemistry, swipe, match, singles, find
love, your person, meant to be.

**Matrimony vocabulary:** biodata, alliance, proposal, caste, community, horoscope,
settled.

**Pity vocabulary:** broken, damaged, failed marriage, starting over from
nothing, second-hand, moving on.

**Growth-hacking vocabulary:** unlock, boost, upgrade, limited time, don't miss
out, exclusive, hurry.

Never a heart — as an icon, an emoji, or a metaphor.

## Naming the audience

Say "divorced, separated and widowed" directly. Avoid euphemism ("people in
transition", "life's next stage") — euphemism reads as embarrassment, and this
audience has had enough of being talked around.

Do not assume the emotional state. Someone may be grieving, relieved, angry, or
long past it. Copy addresses readiness, never feelings we cannot know.

## Claims

Every claim on the page must be one we can defend on launch day.

**Allowed** — bounded, verifiable statements of what the product does:

- "Every member confirms a working phone number and email address."
- "Our team reviews profiles before they go live."
- "Nobody can start a conversation with you unless you have chosen to open it."
- "Every report is read by a person."

**Not allowed** — absolute or unverifiable claims:

- "100% verified" · "Zero fake profiles" · "Completely safe"
- "Guaranteed genuine members"
- Any statistic we cannot source.
- Any member count, testimonial or success story. There are no members yet.

The test: could a journalist check it on launch day and find it true? If not, it
does not ship.

## Competitors

Criticise practices, never companies. "No blurred faces, no 'someone liked you'
nudges designed to make you upgrade" describes a practice. Naming a competitor
would make Eraya sound aggrieved, and the audience is not looking for a fight —
they are looking for somewhere calm.

## Writing for a 40+ reader

- Nothing depends on knowing an app convention.
- Buttons say what happens: "Begin your journey", not "Get started".
- Form labels are questions in plain language: "Where do you live?"
- Errors say what to do: "Please enter your name", not "Invalid input".
- No copy is essential only in a tooltip or a hover state.

## Punctuation

En dashes with spaces for asides ( — ), curly quotes and apostrophes, Indian
English spelling conventions, sentence case for headings and buttons. Numbers
under ten are spelled out except in the step markers (`01`, `02`, `03`).
